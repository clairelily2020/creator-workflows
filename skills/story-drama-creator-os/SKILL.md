# Story Drama Creator OS

Use this skill to turn a raw story idea into a production-ready structure.

## Inputs

- Raw idea
- Target audience
- Format: short drama, comic, vertical video, web series, or social mini-series
- Episode count or expected length
- Tone and genre
- Production constraints

## Workflow

1. **Premise**
   - Write one logline.
   - Identify the emotional engine.
   - Name the audience and why they would care.

2. **Characters**
   - Create a character grid: role, desire, wound, secret, relationship pressure.

3. **Series arc**
   - Break the story into episodes or chapters.
   - Each episode needs a hook, reversal, and ending beat.

4. **Scene cards**
   - Create scene cards for the first episode or requested segment.
   - Include setting, action, conflict, dialogue function, and visual asset needs.

5. **Production checklist**
   - List images, references, locations, props, voice, music, subtitles, and platform assets.

## Quality Gates

- Do not make every character speak the same way.
- Do not create a premise without conflict.
- Do not skip production constraints.
- For AI video/comic workflows, separate writing decisions from visual asset generation.
