# Story Drama Creator OS

This collection is for short drama, comic, mini-series, and AI video creators who need a repeatable story production workflow.

## Jobs

1. Turn a raw idea into a logline.
2. Define audience, genre, hook, and emotional engine.
3. Create a character grid.
4. Break the idea into episodes or chapters.
5. Create scene cards.
6. Produce a visual and asset checklist for production.

## Example Prompt

```text
Use story-drama-creator-os.
Idea: an AI assistant starts managing a creator's life, then develops taste.
Format: 12 short episodes.
Audience: AI creators and tech workers.
Output: logline, character grid, episode arc, scene cards, and asset checklist.
```
